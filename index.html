Install slowed.lol on your Flipper Zero

Custom firmware for Flipper Zero

Everything happens in your browser: the package is downloaded, unpacked and pushed to the SD card over USB. No DFU mode, no extra tools, your data stays on the card.

⚠️ This browser has no WebSerial support, so the one-click installer is unavailable. Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera — or install manually with qFlipper (see below).

  1. 1 Plug in your Flipper

    Use a data-capable USB-C cable and keep an SD card inserted. The installer talks to the Flipper CLI, so any firmware (including the stock one) works as a starting point.

  2. 2 Check the device

    Nothing connected yet.

  3. 3 Choose a build

    Handy for offline builds or when the download is blocked. Grab flipper-z-f7-update-*.tgz from the releases page.

  4. 4 Install

    The package is uploaded to /ext/update and the device flashes itself after a reboot. Don't unplug anything while it works.

Notes & troubleshooting

                

                    
The device is not detected

                    

                        Check the cable (charge-only cables do not work), try another USB port, and
                        quit qFlipper/the mobile app so the CLI is free.
                    

                    
"This Flipper is in DFU/recovery mode"

                    

                        Unplug it, wait a second and plug it back in without touching any button.
                        Then connect again.
                    

                    
The SD card is missing

                    

                        The installer needs a FAT32 card with at least ~10 MiB free. The device can
                        format one itself: Settings → Storage → Format SD card.
                    

                    
Wrong hardware target

                    

                        Builds are published per target (f7 and f18). The
                        installer reads the target from your device and only offers matching
                        packages.
                    

                    
Where is the source?

                    

                        Firmware, release workflow and this installer live in
                        the repository.
